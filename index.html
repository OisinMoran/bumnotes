About

Bum Notes is a tightly-knit improvised musical troupe based in Dublin that have been performing sell-out shows roughly once a month since the start of 2024. We debuted at the Scene + Heard festival of new work, and have performed at the Dublin Fringe Festival (twice), the Edinburgh Improv Festival, and even Electric Picnic. Our most frequented venue is the wonderful Smock Alley Theatre, but we've also trod the boards of The Sugar Club, and even put on Ireland's biggest ever homegrown fully-improvised show to a sold out Royal Irish Academy of Music.

We begin every show by taking a suggestion from the audience. This can be in the form of just the title for a musical that doesn't exist (our standard format), an interview with an audience member (for our "You: The Improvised Musical", Dublin Fringe 2024), a year (for our "Bum Notes' Eras Tour", Dublin Fringe 2025), or a whole bunch of stuff (for our "Pick 'n' Mix" kids show we also did for Dublin Fringe 2025). We then make up an entire musical on the spot based around what we've been given. All the characters, lyrics, melodies, plot, harmonies, and occasional dance numbers are all fully improvised. They have never been seen nor heard before, and never will be again.

Contact

If you'd like to get in touch about working together we'd love to hear from you! We can craft a musical on the spot just for you, with a private show (perhaps celebrating a person or relationship), a corporate gig (where we ask the audience some questions about what your company does, who your competitors are etc. and make up a whole musical about it all), or something else entirely—just ask!
